Partitioning

Scan-to-scan p-ratio

p-ratio = change in fat-free mass ÷ change in total mass. Above 0.5 means most of what you added was lean. This is measured only at scans; everything between them is interpolation.

Why the bike, not the run

Modality is not neutral during a hypertrophy block.

Whole-muscle hypertrophy is not measurably impaired by concurrent aerobic work at typical doses — the pooled estimate is essentially zero. At the muscle-fiber level a small attenuation does appear, and in subgroup analysis it reaches significance for type I fibers when the aerobic mode is running, but not cycling. Eccentric loading and the resulting muscle damage are the likely mechanism.
Schumann M, et al. Sports Med 2022;52(3):601–612 — systematic review and meta-analysis, 43 studies; hypertrophy SMD −0.01 (95% CI −0.16 to 0.18), p = 0.919. Lundberg TR, Feuerbacher JF, Sünkeler M, Schumann M. Sports Med 2022;52(10):2391–2403 — fiber-level meta-analysis; combined type I + II SMD −0.20, p = 0.05; interference significant for type I with running, not cycling. Authors declare no conflicts.
Applied: keep the 4×4 on the bike or the erg. The cost to this block is close to zero. It is the triathlon's swim and long-run volume — the energy cost and the fatigue, not the aerobic signal itself — that was paying for itself in lost lean mass.

The surplus-size evidence, in full

Two randomized trials address this question directly. Both point the same way.

Larger surplus bought fat, not leanRCT · n=39
Elite athletes randomized to nutritional counselling with a planned positive energy balance versus ad libitum intake, over 8–12 weeks, with four added strength sessions per week in both arms. Intake was 3,585 ± 601 vs 2,964 ± 884 kcal. Body mass rose 3.9 ± 0.6% vs 1.5 ± 0.4%, and fat mass rose 15 ± 4% vs 3 ± 3%. Gain in lean body mass did not differ between groups. One-rep-max improved 6–12% in both arms.
Garthe I, Raastad T, Refsnes PE, Sundgot-Borgen J. Eur J Sport Sci 2013;13(3):295–303.
Replicated in trained lifters, at small nRCT · n=21 (17 completed)
Trained lifters randomized to maintenance, 5% surplus, or 15% surplus for eight weeks of supervised training three days per week, with skinfolds, squat and bench 1RM, and ultrasound muscle thickness at biceps, triceps and quadriceps. Faster body-mass gain predicted greater skinfold thickness; it did not predict greater muscle thickness or 1RM. The training stimulus alone produced the strength gains.
Helms ER, Spence A-J, Sousa C, et al. Sports Med Open 2023;9(1):102. Caveat: roughly 5–6 completers per arm after COVID-related attrition — badly underpowered for between-group physiological endpoints. Directionally consistent with Garthe, not independently confirmatory.
But a floor existsmeta-analysis
Energy deficiency during resistance training impairs lean-mass accrual (ES −0.57, p = 0.02) while leaving strength gains statistically intact (ES −0.31, p = 0.28). Undereating is not a safe hedge against fat gain during a hypertrophy block.
Murphy C, Koehler K. Scand J Med Sci Sports 2022;32(1):125–137.

Graded interventions

two axes held separate — strength of the human literature, and the recommendation for this block
Does the workLIT A · FOR YOU A
Progressive set volume, 12–20 direct-equivalent sets per muscle per week67 studies · n=2,058
Multi-level meta-regression of 220 hypertrophy effects and 490 strength effects, with sets classified as direct or indirect before pooling. Hypertrophy rises with weekly set volume across the studied range with diminishing returns; strength shows steeper diminishing returns. Frequency has little independent effect on hypertrophy once weekly volume is equated — so session count is a scheduling convenience, not a stimulus variable.
Pelland JC, Remmert JF, Robinson ZP, Hinson SR, Zourdos MC. Sports Med 2026;56(2):481–505 (epub 2025 Dec 4).
For you: this is the entire lever. The volume ledger above is the instrument. Anything else on this page is a rounding error next to holding 12–20 direct-equivalent sets per muscle with documented load progression.
Creatine monohydrate 5 g/dayalready running since Jan 2026
No change, and no washout. Note the measurement consequence rather than the physiological one: creatine loading raises serum creatinine and depresses creatinine-based eGFR, which is why cystatin C belongs on any renal assessment during this block.
For you: continue. Nothing to decide.
Worth getting right, small marginLIT A · FOR YOU B
Protein 1.6–2.2 g/kg/day49 RCTs · n=1,863
Protein supplementation increased fat-free mass by 0.30 kg (95% CI 0.09–0.52) and 1RM by 2.49 kg (0.64–4.33) across trials of at least six weeks. Two-phase break-point analysis put the plateau at 1.62 g/kg/day, with the confidence interval running to 2.20. The effect was larger in resistance-trained participants and smaller with advancing age — both of which describe you. Conflict disclosed: a 2020 correction to this paper records that one author served on the advisory board of a sports-supplement manufacturer while it was written. The finding has broad independent support; the disclosure is noted because it was omitted originally.
Morton RW, Murphy KT, McKellar SR, et al. Br J Sports Med 2018;52(6):376–384; correction Br J Sports Med 2020;54(19):e7.
For you: at 75 kg the plateau is roughly 122 g and the upper bound of the interval is roughly 165 g. Your standing target of 175 g/day sits above the top of that interval. It is not harmful and it is not wrong — but it is doing no measurable additional work, and it displaces carbohydrate that has a real job during a high-volume block. Treat 150–165 g as the target and put the difference into carbohydrate.
Conditional on measurementLIT B · FOR YOU C
Anything added to the stack before the next drawstanding rule
The rule that governs the frozen stack applies unchanged here. A compound introduced mid-block that shifts an inflammatory or lipid marker does not merely confound the panel; it makes a training-driven change attributable to the compound, permanently.
For you: defer every addition past the draw. If lean gain stalls in this block, the cause will be sets, load progression, or energy — in that order — and no supplement substitutes for a missing training input.

Scope. This page is an instrument, not a clinical record and not medical advice. Between-scan fat and fat-free mass are modelled from body mass and an assumed partitioning ratio, and are labelled as estimates wherever they appear — only scan rows are measurements. Bioimpedance carries its own error; the case for it here rests on standardized serial conditions, not on absolute accuracy. Trial results are quoted with design, n and the pre-specified endpoint so they can be graded rather than taken on trust.
