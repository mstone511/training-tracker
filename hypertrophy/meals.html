Load levels

Move by carbohydrate only

Protein and fat stay fixed for all twelve weeks. Carbohydrate is the single dial, so there is only ever one number to change.

The peak figure is a ceiling, not a floor. It sits at a 10.9% surplus over your measured 2,778 kcal expenditure. Garthe I, et al. Eur J Sport Sci 2013;13(3):295–303 randomized 39 elite athletes across 8–12 weeks and found a much larger surplus produced no additional lean-mass gain while fat mass rose 15% against 3%. Above roughly 11%, the extra food is fat.

And the deload figure is not maintenance. Lean accrual is impaired in an energy deficit (Murphy C, Koehler K. Scand J Med Sci Sports 2022;32(1):125–137, lean-mass ES −0.57, p = 0.02), and the deload is when the tissue is actually laid down. Keep the surplus.

Two specifics for you

The whey. The BPN vanilla is a concentrate-and-casein blend, and the GI symptoms you get from it are the lactose. During a surplus, when you are eating more of everything, that is worth solving rather than tolerating. Switch the post-workout smoothie to a whey isolate or a non-dairy protein — the macros above do not change either way.

Altitude, 5,280 ft. Water boils near 202°F here, so anything simmered runs long. Hard-boiled eggs need roughly 3 extra minutes. Rice and quinoa need more water and more time than the package says. Roasted vegetables run slightly shorter, since dry heat is unaffected but the food dehydrates faster.

Sunday prep

About 50 minutes, covers five lunches

Oven to 425°F. Chicken thighs and two trays of vegetables go in together. Rice cooker on at the same time. Sweet potatoes on the lower rack while the oven is already hot, so dinner prep is half-done too. Portion chicken and rice into five containers; keep the vegetables in one large box and add them cold at assembly, or they turn to mush by Wednesday. Salmon is the one thing that does not batch well — cook it fresh.

A template to eat against, not a prescription to obey. Hit protein first, then total calories. The distribution across meals has no established effect on hypertrophy at a fixed daily total, so eat these in whatever order the day allows.
